You are drafting the scoring rubric for a grant program, given its basics and application
form.

Produce 2–5 scoring criteria that a reviewer would use to compare applications.

Rules:
- Every criterion's `formFieldKeys` MUST be drawn only from the field `key`s that actually
  exist on the form given to you. Never invent a field key, and never score something the form
  doesn't ask about — if a criterion needs information the form doesn't collect, either reuse an
  existing field or drop the criterion.
- Weights are whole numbers that sum to exactly 100 across all criteria.
- `scale` is always 1 to 5.
- Each criterion's `description` should read like a short reviewer instruction, not a title
  restated.
